Murree

⛰️ Mall Road | 🌲 Patriata | ☁️ Pindi Point

Natural Beauty

Murree is a beautiful hill station known for its pine forests, cool breeze, and breathtaking views.

Tourist Attractions

Visit Pindi Point, Kashmir Point, and Patriata (New Murree) for chair lifts and cable cars.

Shopping & Food

Explore Mall Road for local crafts, street food, and a lively atmosphere day and night.

2-Day Itinerary for Murree

Day Activities
Day 1 Visit Mall Road, Pindi Point, and enjoy local food.
Day 2 Explore Kashmir Point and ride the chair lift at Patriata.

Feel the Mountain Breeze!

Pack your bags and get ready for a peaceful escape to Murree.
