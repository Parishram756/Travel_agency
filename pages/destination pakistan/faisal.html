Faisal Mosque

🕌 Largest Mosque | 🏞️ Margalla Hills Backdrop | 🧕 Islamic Architecture

Architectural Marvel

Faisal Mosque features a unique tent-like design with no dome, symbolizing a desert Bedouin tent.

Scenic Location

Located at the foot of the Margalla Hills, it offers a stunning view and peaceful surroundings.

Historic Importance

Gifted by King Faisal of Saudi Arabia, it's the national mosque of Pakistan and a global Islamic icon.

2-Day Visit Plan to Faisal Mosque Area

Day Activities
Day 1 Explore Faisal Mosque, walk through the gardens, visit the prayer halls.
Day 2 Hike in Margalla Hills, visit nearby viewpoints, and relax in the peaceful ambiance.
