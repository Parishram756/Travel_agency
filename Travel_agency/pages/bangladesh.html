Top Attractions in Bangladesh

Discover the natural beauty and rich heritage of Bangladesh. Click any destination to explore details.

Sundarbans

Largest mangrove forest in the world, home to the Royal Bengal Tiger and diverse wildlife.

Cox's Bazar

One of the longest natural sea beaches in the world with golden sands and clear waters.

Srimangal

Tea capital of Bangladesh with endless lush green tea gardens and plantations.

Lalbagh Fort

Historic Mughal-era fort in Dhaka with beautiful architecture and gardens.

Mahasthangarh

Ancient archaeological site dating back to the 3rd century BCE, one of Bangladesh's oldest cities.

Ahsan Manzil

19th-century pink royal palace turned museum in Dhaka, known as the Pink Palace.

St. Martin's Island

Small coral island in the Bay of Bengal with beautiful beaches and marine life.
