Dhorpatan Hunting Reserve

🏞️ Alpine Meadows & Wildlife Trails | 🥁 Magar & Thakali Village Life | ⛺ Rustic Village Lodges

Dhorpatan, tucked away in the western hills of Nepal, is the country's only hunting reserve and a paradise for nature lovers. With sweeping grasslands, alpine forests, and snow-capped peaks in the distance, Dhorpatan offers a rare blend of wilderness and cultural charm. Home to diverse wildlife like blue sheep and Himalayan tahr, as well as traditional villages of the Magar and Thakali people, it’s a perfect escape for trekking, camping, and immersing yourself in unspoiled Himalayan beauty.

Top Experiences

Dhorpatan Hunting Reserve

The only hunting reserve in Nepal, known for its high-altitude grasslands and rich wildlife. A haven for photographers, nature enthusiasts, and those seeking rare Himalayan fauna.

Trekking Through Alpine Meadows

Enjoy peaceful hikes across vast meadows dotted with wildflowers, surrounded by stunning mountain backdrops. The trails pass through remote villages and quiet forests.

Magar & Thakali Village Life

Stay in traditional houses, taste authentic meals, and learn about the customs of the Magar and Thakali people. Experience evenings filled with folk songs and warm hospitality.

Dhorpatan's Itinerary

Day Activities
Day 1 Arrive in Burtibang or nearby entry point from Baglung or Rukum. Short hike to the edge of the reserve, exploring riverside trails and alpine views. Evening relaxation in a village homestay.
Day 2 Begin the day with a trek deep into Dhorpatan Hunting Reserve. Spot wildlife like blue sheep, pheasants, and Himalayan tahr. Picnic lunch by the meadows. Return to camp or lodge in the evening.
Day 3 Explore remote Magar and Thakali villages, learning about their lifestyle and traditions. Afternoon free for photography and short hikes to nearby viewpoints.
Day 4 Final morning walk to capture sunrise over the mountains. Return journey via Baglung, Rukum, or Pokhara, taking in the scenic drives along the way.
