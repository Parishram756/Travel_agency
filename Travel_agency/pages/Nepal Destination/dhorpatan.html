Dhorpatan Hunting Reserve

🏔 Alpine Meadows & Mountain Views | 🐏 Himalayan Blue Sheep Spotting | 🏕 Remote Camping Adventures

Dhorpatan Hunting Reserve, Nepal's only hunting reserve, is a remote high-altitude paradise stretching across Rukum, Baglung, and Myagdi districts. Known for its wide alpine meadows, snow-capped mountain backdrops, and rare Himalayan blue sheep, the reserve offers an authentic off-the-beaten-path trekking experience. Visitors can enjoy camping under starry skies, exploring diverse wildlife habitats, and interacting with local Magar communities.

Top Experiences

Blue Sheep Spotting

Hike into the reserve's high pastures to catch a glimpse of the elusive Himalayan blue sheep grazing against a backdrop of towering peaks.

Alpine Trekking

Explore the pristine trails through pine forests, meadows, and rugged mountain passes with panoramic views of Dhaulagiri and other ranges.

Magar Cultural Encounters

Stay in remote Magar villages, savor traditional meals, and learn about the customs and lifestyle of this warm and welcoming community.

Dhorpatan Itinerary

Day Activities
Day 1 Arrive in Burtibang or Rukumkot via road. Begin trek towards the reserve with a scenic hike through terraced hillsides and forests.
Day 2 Trek into the alpine zone, spotting blue sheep and birds along the way. Set up camp in the meadows of Dhorpatan.
Day 3 Explore high pastures and enjoy mountain panoramas. Visit nearby Magar settlements and experience their hospitality.
Day 4 Return trek to the starting point and depart for your next destination.
