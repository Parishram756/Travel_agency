Mustang

🏜️ Desert Cliffs & Cliffside Trails | 🕉️ Tibetan Culture & Monasteries | 🏯 Traditional Mustang Homestays

Mustang, often called the “Last Forbidden Kingdom,” lies in Nepal's trans-Himalayan region, where Tibetan culture thrives amid dramatic desert landscapes. Split into Lower and Upper Mustang, it offers a surreal mix of red cliffs, ancient caves, monasteries, and snowy peaks. From the vibrant town of Jomsom to the walled city of Lo Manthang, Mustang is a journey through time, culture, and breathtaking Himalayan beauty.

Top Experiences

Lo Manthang

Explore the ancient walled capital of Upper Mustang with its centuries-old monasteries, mud-brick houses, and unique Tibetan-influenced culture.

Kagbeni

A picturesque medieval village at the gateway to Upper Mustang, offering stunning views, traditional architecture, and the famous Kagbeni Monastery.

Sky Caves of Mustang

Marvel at mysterious cliffside caves used for centuries as dwellings, burial chambers, and meditation retreats by ancient Himalayan communities.

Mustang's Itinerary

Day Activities
Day 1 Fly or drive from Pokhara to Jomsom. Explore the windy town, visit local markets, and enjoy the stunning Kali Gandaki river valley views.
Day 2 Drive or trek to Kagbeni, the gateway to Upper Mustang. Wander through narrow alleys, visit Kag Chode Thupten Samphel Ling Monastery, and take in panoramic views.
Day 3 Head towards Lo Manthang, exploring its royal palace, monasteries, and local art. In the evening, enjoy a cultural performance with traditional Tibetan music and dance.
Day 4 Visit the Sky Caves and nearby viewpoints before returning to Jomsom. Evening free to relax and soak in Mustang's unique desert-like scenery.
