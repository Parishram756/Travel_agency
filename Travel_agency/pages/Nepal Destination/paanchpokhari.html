Paanchpokhari

🏔️ Sacred High-Altitude Treks | 🙏 Pilgrimage Festivals & Traditions | 🛖 Mountain Camps & Lodges

Paanch Pokhari, meaning "Five Lakes," is a sacred and breathtaking destination in Sindhupalchok district of Nepal. Nestled at an altitude of about 4,100 meters, this tranquil cluster of alpine lakes is surrounded by snow-capped peaks, lush meadows, and spiritual charm. It is a revered pilgrimage site during Janai Purnima, attracting both devotees and trekkers seeking untouched natural beauty and cultural significance.

Top Experiences

The Five Sacred Lakes

Marvel at the crystal-clear waters of the five alpine lakes, reflecting surrounding mountains and skies. A peaceful haven for meditation and photography.

Pilgrimage During Janai Purnima

Experience the vibrant cultural gathering where thousands of devotees trek to the lakes to offer prayers, creating a colorful and spiritual atmosphere.

Himalayan Panorama

Enjoy stunning views of Jugal Himal, Dorje Lakpa, and the Langtang range from nearby ridges and passes, especially at sunrise and sunset.

Paanch Pokhari's Itinerary

Day Activities
Day 1 Drive from Kathmandu to Chautara, the starting point of the trek. Begin the trek with a gradual ascent through terraced fields and small villages. Overnight in a local lodge or campsite.
Day 2 Trek through dense rhododendron and pine forests, enjoying mountain views along the way. Stop in alpine meadows for lunch before reaching a scenic campsite near Tupi Danda.
Day 3 Reach Paanch Pokhari, exploring all five lakes and their surrounding viewpoints. Spend the afternoon soaking in the serene atmosphere and capturing the stunning reflections in the water.
Day 4 Early morning hike to a ridge for panoramic Himalayan views, then begin the descent back toward Chautara. Depart for Kathmandu in the evening.
