Kathmandu

🕌 Ancient Temples & Heritage Sites | 🛍 Vibrant Bazaars | 🍜 Street Food & Local Flavors

Kathmandu, the capital city of Nepal, is a vibrant mix of ancient heritage, bustling streets, and rich cultural traditions. Home to UNESCO World Heritage Sites like Swayambhunath, Pashupatinath, and Durbar Square, it offers a perfect blend of history, spirituality, and modern life. From wandering through narrow alleys filled with colorful shops to enjoying rooftop views over centuries-old temples, Kathmandu is an unmissable stop on any Nepal journey.

Top Experiences

Explore Durbar Squares

Visit the historical Durbar Squares of Kathmandu, Patan, and Bhaktapur to admire Newar architecture, palaces, and courtyards.

Spiritual Journeys

Experience peace at Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple), Boudhanath Stupa, and the sacred Pashupatinath Temple on the Bagmati River.

Thamel Nightlife & Markets

Shop for handicrafts, try local delicacies, and enjoy live music in the vibrant streets of Thamel.

Kathmandu Itinerary

Day Activities
Day 1 Arrive in Kathmandu. Evening stroll in Thamel and enjoy Nepali dinner.
Day 2 Visit Swayambhunath, Pashupatinath, and Boudhanath Stupa. Explore Kathmandu Durbar Square in the afternoon.
Day 3 Day trip to Patan and Bhaktapur for heritage tours. Try local Newari cuisine.
Day 4 Morning shopping for souvenirs before departure to your next destination.
