Kathmandu Valley

🏛️ Heritage Walks & City Trails | 🎉 Festivals & Local Culture | 🏨 Boutique Hotels & Cultural Stays

Kathmandu, Nepal's vibrant capital, is a city where ancient heritage and bustling modern life blend seamlessly. Known as the “City of Temples,” it's home to UNESCO World Heritage Sites, colorful markets, and lively streets filled with the aroma of local cuisine. From spiritual landmarks to cultural festivals, Kathmandu offers a captivating introduction to Nepal's history, art, and traditions.

Top Experiences

Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple)

Perched atop a hill, this iconic stupa offers panoramic city views and a deep sense of spiritual peace, alongside playful monkeys.

Pashupatinath Temple

One of the holiest Hindu temples in the world, situated on the banks of the Bagmati River, rich with rituals, history, and devotion.

Durbar Square

A stunning collection of palaces, courtyards, and temples that showcase Newar architecture and centuries of royal history.

Kathmandu's Itinerary

Day Activities
Day 1 Arrive in Kathmandu and take a walking tour of Thamel's vibrant streets, trying local delicacies like momo and Newari snacks.
Day 2 Visit Swayambhunath in the morning for sunrise views, then explore Kathmandu Durbar Square. Evening at a cultural dinner show.
Day 3 Spend the day visiting Pashupatinath Temple, Boudhanath Stupa, and the bustling Asan market. Try traditional Nepali tea and sweets.
Day 4 Optional day trip to Bhaktapur and Patan for more UNESCO heritage sites before departing from Kathmandu.
