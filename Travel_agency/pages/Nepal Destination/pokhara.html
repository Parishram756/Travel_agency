Pokhara

🛶 Lakeside Treks & Mountain Views | 🎨 Local Traditions & Lakeside Culture | 🏡 Cozy Lakeside Stays

Pokhara, Nepal's lake city, is a dreamlike blend of natural beauty and adventure. Nestled beside the tranquil Phewa Lake and backed by the majestic Annapurna range, Pokhara is a paradise for trekkers, paragliders, and nature lovers. From sunrise over the Himalayas to serene boat rides, vibrant lakeside streets, and cascading waterfalls, Pokhara promises experiences that touch both heart and soul. With warm hospitality and endless activities, it’s the perfect escape for relaxation and exploration.

Top Experiences

Phewa Lake

Enjoy a peaceful boat ride on Pokhara's famous lake with the Annapurna peaks reflecting on the water. Visit Tal Barahi Temple in the middle of the lake for a unique spiritual experience.

World Peace Pagoda

Perched on a hilltop, the pagoda offers panoramic views of Pokhara city, Phewa Lake, and the snow-capped mountains. A perfect spot for sunrise or sunset photography.

Paragliding in Sarangkot

Soar above Pokhara's valleys and lakes with the Himalayas as your backdrop. Sarangkot is one of the world's top paragliding spots for thrill seekers.

Pokhara's Itinerary

Day Activities
Day 1 Arrive in Pokhara by flight or bus from Kathmandu. Spend the afternoon strolling around Lakeside, enjoying coffee by Phewa Lake. In the evening, take a short boat ride to Tal Barahi Temple and watch the sunset over the lake.
Day 2 Early morning trip to Sarangkot for sunrise views of the Annapurna range. Return for breakfast, then enjoy paragliding or zip-lining. Afternoon visit to Davis Falls and Gupteshwor Cave. Evening leisure at Lakeside.
Day 3 Take a morning hike or short drive to the World Peace Pagoda. Afternoon visit to the International Mountain Museum and Bat Cave. Relax in the evening with a lakeside dinner and live music.
Day 4 Enjoy a morning boat ride and optional paddleboarding on Phewa Lake. Explore local souvenir shops before departure to Kathmandu or other destinations.
