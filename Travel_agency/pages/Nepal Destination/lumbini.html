Lumbini

🌿 Peaceful Pilgrimage Walks | 🕯️ Buddhist Rituals & Traditions | 🏘️ Tranquil Guesthouses & Retreats

Lumbini, the birthplace of Lord Buddha, is one of the most sacred pilgrimage sites in the world. Located in Nepal's Rupandehi district, it offers a serene and spiritual atmosphere with monasteries, meditation gardens, and historical landmarks. Recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, Lumbini attracts visitors seeking peace, history, and a deeper connection to Buddhist heritage.

Top Experiences

Maya Devi Temple

The heart of Lumbini, marking the exact spot where Buddha was born. Explore ancient ruins, the sacred pond, and archaeological treasures.

Monastic Zone

A peaceful area filled with stunning monasteries built by countries around the world, each reflecting unique cultural and architectural styles.

Ashokan Pillar

An ancient sandstone pillar erected by Emperor Ashoka in 249 BC, standing as a testament to Lumbini's historical and spiritual significance.

Lumbini's Itinerary

Day Activities
Day 1 Arrive in Lumbini by road or flight via Bhairahawa. Visit the Maya Devi Temple and explore the sacred pond and surrounding gardens.
Day 2 Tour the Monastic Zone, visiting monasteries built by countries like Thailand, China, Japan, and Germany. Enjoy peaceful meditation spots.
Day 3 Visit the Ashokan Pillar, Lumbini Museum, and World Peace Pagoda. Spend the evening in the tranquil meditation gardens.
Day 4 Morning meditation or yoga session. Depart for nearby attractions such as Tilaurakot, the ancient capital of the Shakya Kingdom.
