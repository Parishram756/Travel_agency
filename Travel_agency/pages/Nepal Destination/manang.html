Manang

❄️ Annapurna Circuit & Glacier Hikes | 🧘 Tibetan-Influenced Culture | 🛏️ Teahouse Stays & Himalayan Hospitality

Manang, nestled high in the Annapurna region at over 3,500 meters, is a Himalayan paradise for trekkers and adventure seekers. Surrounded by snow-capped peaks, turquoise glacial lakes, and traditional stone villages, Manang is a highlight of the Annapurna Circuit. The region blends Tibetan-influenced culture, breathtaking scenery, and outdoor activities, making it an unforgettable stop in Nepal’s high Himalayas.

Top Experiences

Tilicho Lake

One of the highest lakes in the world, Tilicho is a shimmering turquoise jewel set against dramatic snow peaks — a must-visit for trekkers.

Gangapurna Lake & Glacier

A short walk from Manang village, this lake offers spectacular reflections of surrounding mountains and views of Gangapurna Glacier.

Thorong La Pass Preparation

Acclimatize in Manang before tackling the iconic Thorong La Pass. Explore nearby monasteries, viewpoints, and day hikes to prepare for the ascent.

Manang's Itinerary

Day Activities
Day 1 Trek or drive from Chame to Manang, enjoying views of Annapurna II, III, and Gangapurna. Settle into a teahouse and explore the village streets in the evening.
Day 2 Day hike to Gangapurna Lake and surrounding viewpoints. Visit the Himalayan Rescue Association for acclimatization advice.
Day 3 Full-day trek to Tilicho Lake (or start a two-day Tilicho trip). For shorter options, hike to Ice Lake or Praken Gompa for spectacular panoramic views.
Day 4 Acclimatization hike before departing towards Thorong La Pass. Visit local monasteries and enjoy the peaceful Himalayan surroundings.
