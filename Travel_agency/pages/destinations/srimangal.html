Srimangal

🍵 Tea Capital of Bangladesh | 🌿 Lush Green Tea Gardens | 🐦 Bird Watching Paradise

Srimangal, known as the "Tea Capital of Bangladesh," is located in the Moulvibazar district of Sylhet division. This picturesque region is famous for its endless tea gardens that stretch across rolling hills, creating a sea of vibrant green. Srimangal is home to more than 150 tea gardens and several tea factories, making it the heart of Bangladesh's tea industry. Beyond tea, Srimangal offers natural beauty, wildlife sanctuaries, and tribal culture.

Top Experiences

Tea Garden Tour

Visit working tea gardens, learn about tea cultivation and processing, and enjoy fresh tea tasting sessions with panoramic views of the plantations.

Barachapi Hill

Climb to the top of Barachapi, the highest peak in Srimangal, for breathtaking views of the surrounding tea gardens and valleys.

Madhabpur Lake

Enjoy boating on this serene lake surrounded by tea gardens and forests, especially beautiful during the lotus blossom season.

2-Day Srimangal Itinerary

Day Activities
Day 1 Arrive in Srimangal, visit 4 tea gardens (Manus, Malnichhara, Somer, and Haripur), tea tasting session, sunset view at Barachapi Hill
Day 2 Morning visit to Lawachara National Park, explore Madhabpur Lake, visit tea factory, return with scenic views
