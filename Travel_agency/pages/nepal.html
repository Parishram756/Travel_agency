Top Attractions in Nepal

Explore the astonishing beauty of Nepal, The country gifted with natural landscapes and spiritual heritages.

Chitwan

Home to wildlife safaris and Chitwan National Park

Dhorpatan

Only hunting reserve in Nepal with alpine beauty.

Kathmandu

Capital city rich in culture, temples, and history.

Lumbini

Birthplace of Lord Buddha and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Manang

Himalayan valley known for trekking and mountain vistas.

Mustang

Ancient kingdom with desert-like terrain and Tibetan culture.

Paanchpokhari

Sacred pilgrimage site with five holy lakes nestled in the Himalayas.

Pokhara

Scenic city with lakes, caves, and Himalayan views.

Rukum East/Rukum West

Culturally rich, remote districts with rugged landscapes.
